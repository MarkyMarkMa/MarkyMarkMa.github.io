## Overview

Direct long-horizon forecasting asks a model to predict an entire future window in a single step, even though uncertainty generally grows with the forecasting horizon.

This work introduces **Evolutionary Forecasting**, summarized as “train near, infer far.” During training, the model learns from shorter, higher-confidence prediction windows. At inference time, it extrapolates progressively in blocks, allowing one model to operate across longer forecasting horizons.

## Research themes

- Long-term time-series forecasting
- Direct versus progressive forecasting
- Short-window training
- Blockwise extrapolation at inference time
