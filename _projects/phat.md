## Overview

Multivariate time series often contain variables with different periodic patterns. Treating every channel with the same temporal mechanism can obscure this heterogeneity.

PHAT models periodicity as an inductive bias for deep learning. The work characterizes channel-specific temporal patterns and develops feature-extraction and channel-interaction mechanisms tailored to different periodic behaviors, with the goal of improving multivariate time-series forecasting.

## Research themes

- Multivariate time-series forecasting
- Period heterogeneity across variable channels
- Temporal feature extraction
- Channel interaction in deep forecasting models
