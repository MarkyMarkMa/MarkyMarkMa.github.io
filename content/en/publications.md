  <article class="research-entry" id="phat">
    <h2>PHAT: Modeling Period Heterogeneity for Multivariate Time Series Forecasting</h2>
    <p class="research-meta"><span class="research-venue">ICLR 2026 Poster</span><span>Third author</span></p>
    <p>PHAT treats periodicity as an inductive bias for deep learning. It characterizes channel-specific temporal patterns and develops feature-extraction and channel-interaction mechanisms tailored to heterogeneous periodic behaviors in multivariate time series.</p>
    <p class="research-collaboration">In collaboration with the School of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, University of Science and Technology of China.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="research-entry" id="evolutionary-forecasting">
    <h2>To See Far, Look Close: Evolutionary Forecasting for Long-term Time Series</h2>
    <p class="research-meta"><span class="research-venue">Under review</span><span>Fourth author</span></p>
    <p>This work studies the direct forecasting paradigm and introduces Evolutionary Forecasting: “train near, infer far.” The model learns from shorter, higher-confidence prediction windows and progressively extrapolates in blocks at inference time.</p>
    <p class="research-collaboration">In collaboration with the School of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, University of Science and Technology of China.</p>
